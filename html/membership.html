Our membership is non-refundable. You can turn off your auto-renew so you won’t be charged again. If you’d like help getting the most from a membership you’ve purchased, email us at support@farnamstreetblog.com, and we’ll point you in the right direction.

We do not offer a trial membership. Most of our content can be accessed and downloaded as soon as you sign up.
